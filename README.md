# The Family Archive

The shareable copy of a family tree: Golaknath, Mitter, Dutt, Chatterjee, Banerji, Singh, Netram, Mangat Rai.

This site is a single generated page. Living people appear only as a silhouette and initials until
they have agreed to be shown; everything about them stays private until then. The research behind
it - sources, citations, photographs, life stories - is kept offline and is not in this repository.

Every fact on the page carries its source. Corrections are welcome: contact the family member who
shared the link with you.
